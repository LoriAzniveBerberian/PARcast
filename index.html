Lambert’s cosine law

Why a tilted surface gets less light

The same beam spreads over a wider footprint as the angle steepens, so irradiance falls by cos θ. This is the response a cosine-corrected PAR sensor is built to mimic.

At θ = 0° the surface sees full intensity; at 60° it’s halved; near the horizon it fades to nothing. A bare detector under-reads steep angles — the diffuser dome corrects it back onto this curve.
